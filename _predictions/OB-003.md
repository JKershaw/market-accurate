# OB-003: Frontier training <$1M

**Status:** 🟡 Pending

**Headline:** By end of 2026, at least one lab will demonstrate frontier-class model training for under $1M total compute cost.

**Why this matters:** If frontier capability can be reached for <$1M, the compute moat collapses and the AI infrastructure thesis breaks at the training-cost level.

---

## Full claim

By end of 2026, at least one lab will demonstrate frontier-class model training (MMLU >85%) for under $1M total compute cost.

## Threshold

Documented training cost <$1M (with credible methodology, not promotional estimate) for a model achieving MMLU ≥85% by Dec 31, 2026.

## Base rate

Multiple sub-$10M frontier runs reported; strict <$1M is much harder. Base-rate probability: ~30%.
