# SC-003: Equipment vendor YoY decline

**Status:** 🟡 Pending

**Headline:** At least one major semiconductor equipment vendor will report a year-over-year revenue decline in Q3 or Q4 2026.

**Why this matters:** Equipment vendors lead the cycle — a YoY revenue decline would be the cleanest cycle-turn signal absent a memory price collapse.

---

## Full claim

At least one major equipment vendor (ASML, Applied Materials, Lam Research, KLA) will report YoY revenue decline in Q3 or Q4 2026.

## Threshold

Q3 or Q4 2026 quarterly revenue from any of ASML/AMAT/LRCX/KLAC strictly less than corresponding 2025 quarter, per 10-Q filings.

## Base rate

All major vendors showed growth through Q3 2025. Base-rate probability for any single vendor to print a YoY decline within 2 quarters: ~25%.
