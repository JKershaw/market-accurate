# CS-004: 2026 retail sales < NRF 4.4% forecast

**Status:** 🟡 Pending

**Headline:** 2026 US retail sales growth will come in below the NRF's 4.4% forecast.

## Threshold

Census 2026 retail sales annual growth <4.4%.

NRF forecasts have been roughly accurate historically. Base-rate probability of undershooting: ~50%.
