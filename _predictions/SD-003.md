# SD-003: Measured speedup stays sub-2x (expert devs)

**Status:** 🟡 Pending

**Headline:** The next rigorous controlled study of AI coding tools on experienced developers in mature codebases will report a point-estimate speedup below 2x.

**Why this matters:** Tests the '10x engineer' claim against the strongest causal evidence; METR's 2025 RCT found experienced devs were actually slower.

---

## Full claim

The next rigorous controlled study of AI coding tools on experienced developers working in mature codebases will report a point-estimate productivity improvement below 2x.

## Threshold

A METR-class RCT or equivalent peer-reviewed/quasi-experimental study published by Dec 31, 2027 reports a central point-estimate speedup of <100% (i.e., <2x) for experienced developers on mature repositories. Point estimate, not a best-case subgroup. If no qualifying study publishes by the date, resolves INCONCLUSIVE.

## Base rate

METR's July 2025 RCT found −19% (slower); the Feb 2026 follow-up argued for a likely-higher-but-unmeasured speedup. No controlled study to date has shown ≥2x for this cohort. Base-rate probability of <2x: ~78%.
