# AV-007: Hyperscaler absolute capex cut

**Status:** 🟡 Pending

**Headline:** At least one major hyperscaler will reduce absolute AI capex year-over-year by end of 2027.

**Why this matters:** The hard form of the capex moderation thesis — not a growth-rate slowdown but an absolute dollar cut. The clearest possible signal that the efficiency thesis is binding at the spend level.

---

## Full claim

At least one major hyperscaler (Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Meta) will reduce absolute AI capex YoY by end of 2027.

## Threshold

Any of MSFT/GOOG/AMZN/META reports calendar-2027 capital expenditure strictly less than calendar-2026 capex in their official financial filings.

## Base rate

Base-rate probability of any single hyperscaler cutting absolute capex YoY in a non-recession year: ~10%.
