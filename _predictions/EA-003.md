# EA-003: 3+ Fortune 500 disclose AI ROI

**Status:** 🟡 Pending

**Headline:** By Q4 2026, at least 3 Fortune 500 companies will publicly disclose specific dollar AI ROI figures in earnings reports.

**Why this matters:** The opposite-tail test: enterprises proving (not just claiming) AI ROI. If 3+ disclose specific numbers, the bear thesis loses its strongest argument.

---

## Full claim

By Q4 2026, at least 3 Fortune 500 companies will publicly disclose quantified AI ROI metrics in earnings reports (specific dollar figures, not just percentages).

## Threshold

Specific, quantified AI ROI disclosure (with dollar amounts) in 3+ Fortune 500 earnings transcripts or releases by Feb 2027.

## Base rate

As of Jan 2026, very few Fortune 500 disclose specific AI ROI dollars. Base-rate probability: ~30%.
