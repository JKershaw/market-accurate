# SD-005: Aggregate software employment holds

**Status:** 🟡 Pending

**Headline:** US aggregate software-developer employment will not fall more than 10% from its 2024 level through 2026, even as entry-level hiring stays weak.

**Why this matters:** Tests whether AI coding disruption is aggregate or concentrated; the software-specific version of the Labor Market analysis's concentrated-not-aggregate finding.

---

## Full claim

US aggregate software-developer employment will not fall more than 10% from its 2024 level through 2026, even as entry-level hiring stays depressed.

## Threshold

BLS OES occupation 15-1252 'Software Developers' total employment in the 2026 OES release (published ~spring 2027) is ≥90% of the 2024 OES level. Aggregate occupation total (15-1252), not CES or LinkedIn, and explicitly not entry-level hiring flows.

## Base rate

Aggregate software employment has been roughly flat-to-rising despite a sharp entry-level pullback; a >10% aggregate decline in one year would be unprecedented absent a recession. Base-rate probability of ≥90% retention: ~65%.
