# HC-001: Big-4 quarterly capex <$150B

**Status:** 🟡 Pending

**Headline:** No single quarter in 2026 will see combined Big-4 hyperscaler capex exceed $150B.

**Why this matters:** A direct test of whether the unprecedented AI capex trajectory can sustain itself for a full year, or whether ROI pressure or supply constraints force a cap.

---

## Full claim

Combined Big-4 (MSFT/GOOG/AMZN/META) quarterly capex will peak below $150 billion in any quarter of 2026.

## Threshold

No 2026 calendar quarter shows combined MSFT + GOOGL + AMZN + META capex (cash capex including finance leases) ≥$150B per official 10-Q filings.

## Base rate

Q3 2025 combined capex was ~$113B. Annualizing $700B FY26 guidance gives ~$175B avg quarterly. Base-rate probability of staying below $150B in every quarter: ~15%.
