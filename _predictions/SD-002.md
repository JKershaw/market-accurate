# SD-002: No majority-autonomous production codebase

**Status:** 🟡 Pending

**Headline:** Through 2027, no major software org will publicly report that most of its merged production PRs were authored end-to-end by an autonomous AI agent without human edits.

**Why this matters:** Separates autonomous production from AI-assistance; the popular 'engineers are now reviewers of digital labor' narrative requires this and it has not been demonstrated.

---

## Full claim

Through end of 2027, no major software organization will publicly report that a majority of its merged production pull requests were authored end-to-end by an autonomous AI agent without human edit.

## Threshold

No credible disclosure (engineering blog from a top-50-by-revenue software company, SEC filing, or peer-reviewed study) by Dec 31, 2027 stating that >50% of merged production PRs were authored autonomously by AI with no human code edits. 'AI-assisted'/'AI-suggested' lines do not count; human review is allowed but human edits disqualify a PR from the autonomous count.

## Base rate

No such disclosure exists as of mid-2026, and no rigorous public number for autonomous-PR-merge-without-rework exists at all. Base-rate probability the claim (no majority disclosure) holds through 2027: ~70%.
