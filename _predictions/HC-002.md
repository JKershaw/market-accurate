# HC-002: Hyperscaler cuts FY26 guidance

**Status:** 🟡 Pending

**Headline:** At least one hyperscaler will cut their full-year 2026 capex guidance mid-year.

## Base rate

Guidance pattern through Q1 2026 has been monotonically upward. Base-rate probability: ~15%.
