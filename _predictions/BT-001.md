# BT-001: First AI-discovered FDA approval

**Status:** 🟡 Pending

**Headline:** By end of 2027, the first AI-discovered drug will receive FDA approval.

**Why this matters:** The single most important test of whether AI is inflecting Eroom's Law in drug discovery.

---

## Full claim

The first AI-discovered drug (where AI was used for target identification or molecular design) will receive FDA approval by December 31, 2027.

## Threshold

## Base rate

No AI-discovered drug has yet been approved; multiple in late-stage trials. Base-rate probability: ~55%.
